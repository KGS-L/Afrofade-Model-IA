# Story 13.5

Expose SEO-friendly public salon and professional pages using constrained server projections only. A public entity must be `listing_status='published'`, `verification_status='verified'` and commercially entitled where applicable. Public payloads exclude private email, private professional address/coordinates, internal memberships, subscriptions and billing data.

Professional pages expose approved portfolio, normalized skills, services, public affiliations/service area and booking eligibility. Salon pages expose public business identity/address/location, services and public team identity. Reviews/availability are progressive modules supplied by later epics.
